# Health &amp; Safety Disclaimer — Kairos

_Last updated: July 7, 2026_

This disclaimer is part of the Kairos [Terms of Service](terms). **Please read it:
it describes the limits of what the app can do and the responsibility that stays
with you.**

## Read this first

Kairos suggests *times* to be outside based on forecasts and your preferences. It
is a convenience tool for planning, **not a safety device, not a professional
advisor, and not a source of guaranteed information.** Every decision to go
outside, and to start, continue, or stop any activity, is yours. When in doubt,
don't go — or turn back.

## Not medical advice

Kairos does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and is not a
substitute for professional judgment. Its suggestions to be active outdoors are
general lifestyle information, not a prescription tailored to your health.

**Consult a physician before beginning or significantly changing any exercise or
outdoor-activity routine**, especially if you are pregnant, older, have a heart,
lung, or other medical condition, take medication, or have any reason to think
activity may be unsafe for you. If you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath,
or other warning signs during an activity, stop and seek medical help. **In an
emergency, call your local emergency number.**

## The data can be wrong, late, or missing

The app's suggestions are automated estimates built from third-party forecasts —
including Apple Weather (WeatherKit), NOAA / NOS CO-OPS tide predictions, and
Open-Meteo air-quality and marine data. Forecasts are inherently uncertain and
these services can be inaccurate, delayed, incomplete, or unavailable. Conditions
outdoors can change quickly and differ from any forecast, and can vary a lot over
short distances and short times.

**Do not rely on the app as your only source.** Before you go, check current
official sources for where you'll be, and use your own eyes and judgment once
you're there.

## Water, surf, and the coast — extra caution

The ocean is dangerous and unforgiving. Kairos's surf, tide, and beach
information is for general planning only and **must never be used as a water- or
beach-safety authority.** Waves, currents, rip currents, tides, and shorebreak
can injure or kill even in conditions that look mild.

- Heed lifeguards, posted signs, flags, and official **Beach Hazard Statements**
  and marine warnings — they override anything the app shows.
- Know your own limits, never surf or swim alone in unfamiliar or hazardous
  conditions, and be especially careful around tides, rocks, and remote breaks.
- Tide predictions are estimates and do not account for storms, surge, or local
  effects.

## Weather and severe conditions

Kairos is **not an emergency, weather-warning, or safety-of-life service.** It may
not reflect fast-changing or severe weather. For warnings and alerts, rely on
official sources such as the National Weather Service and local authorities, and
follow their guidance over anything in the app.

## Sun, UV, air quality, and eye safety

- Air-quality and UV information is general and may lag real conditions; if you
  are sensitive, follow medical guidance and official air-quality advisories.
- **Never look directly at the sun.** Any sun, sunrise/sunset, or eclipse
  information in the app is for timing and interest only — viewing the sun or a
  solar eclipse without proper certified eye protection can cause permanent eye
  damage. Follow established solar-viewing safety guidance.

## You bring the judgment

The app does not know your fitness, experience, equipment, the specific terrain,
trail or water conditions, wildlife, crowding, lighting, or how remote or
reachable a place is. Assess these yourself, prepare appropriately, tell someone
where you're going, and choose activities and locations within your ability.

## Assumption of risk

Outdoor activities carry inherent risks of injury, illness, property loss, or
death. By using Kairos and choosing to undertake any activity, **you knowingly and
voluntarily accept full responsibility for those risks.** To the fullest extent
permitted by law, you release the developer from liability for any harm arising
from your use of the app or the activities you choose to do, as further described
in the Terms of Service.
